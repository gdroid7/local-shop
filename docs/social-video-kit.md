# ShopDeck — Social Video Kit & Outreach Playbook

This kit provides ready-to-record video templates, storyboards, voiceover scripts, and copy-paste social captions tailored for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Threads.

---

## 📱 Concept 1: "The 30-Tab Group Chat Nightmare" (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

- **Target Audience**: Women, girls, event shoppers, fashion enthusiasts.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 Vertical (`1080x1920` or mobile screen recording).
- **Duration**: 12–15 seconds.
- **Audio Recommendation**: Trending casual upbeat lofi sound, or casual spoken voiceover.

### Storyboard Breakdown

| Timestamp | Visual Screen Action | On-Screen Text Overlay | Voiceover (Optional) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:03** | Close-up of a WhatsApp group chat full of 15 messy blue links with the text *"Girls which heels should I wear with this dress??"* | `POV: You have 30 Safari tabs open trying to pick 1 outfit 😭` | *"If your group chat looks like this every time you have an event to shop for..."* |
| **0:03 – 0:06** | Screen switches to ShopDeck on phone. Paste the copied WhatsApp text into the box -> Tap **"Scrape & Add"**. | `Pasting all 15 links into ShopDeck... ✨` | *"Stop opening 20 different tabs."* |
| **0:06 – 0:11** | Cards load automatically. Switch to **Swipe Mode**. Thumbs swiping right on favorites (`♥`), swiping left on rejects (`✕`). | `It turns your links into a Tinder swipe deck to pick the winner 👠` | *"Just paste them here. It pulls the photos and prices, and lets you swipe through them like Tinder to pick the final one."* |
| **0:11 – 0:15** | Quick flip back to the 2-column Grid view showing the coordinated dress + shoes + bag. | `Free & local-first. Link in bio / GitHub 🛍️` | *"Check the link in bio to try it!"* |

## 📱 Concept 2: "Developer / Indie Hacker Build in Public" (Threads / X / Reddit)

- **Target Audience**: Web developers, open source enthusiasts, product curators.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 Square or 16:9 Landscape.
- **Duration**: 15–20 seconds.

### Post Template for Threads & X

> **Post Copy**:
>
> I got tired of having 40 tabs open across Zara, Myntra, and Amazon whenever shopping for an event.
> 
> So I built **ShopDeck**: an open-source visual shopping shortlist tool.
> 
> - Paste raw links or messy WhatsApp chat dumps
> - Scrapes metadata via JSON-LD + Cheerio
> - Tinder-style swipe UI to pick the winning item
> - Local-first SQLite cache (runs offline)
> 
> Built with React 19, Vite, Express, and Better-SQLite3.
> 
> ⭐ Repo: https://github.com/gdroid7/shopDeck  
> 🔗 Live Demo: https://corliss-unveracious-onward.ngrok-free.dev
> 
> Would love your feedback!

## 🚀 How to Use the Automated Outreach Hub

You now have a local outreach dashboard with one-click direct message launches:

1. Open the interactive dashboard in your browser:
   ```bash
   open outreach/index.html
   ```
2. Filter between:
   - **GitHub & Tech Curators**: Curators on X with 80k–200k+ followers who share daily repos.
   - **Fashion & Lifestyle Stylists**: Curators on Threads / X who discuss outfit selection and capsule closets.
   - **Indie Hacker Curators**: Creators who showcase lightweight problem-solving software.
3. Click **"📋 Copy"** to copy the personalized pitch or **"🐦 Tweet / DM"** to launch Twitter/X directly with the pre-filled message!
4. Click **"Pending"** to toggle to **"✓ Sent"** to track your daily outreach progress.
